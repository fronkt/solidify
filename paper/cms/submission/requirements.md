# Computational Materials Science submission spec (approved by the author 2026-11-27)

Sources: the journal's live Guide for Authors could not be fetched (HTTP 403 to automated clients). Rules below
come from (A) the live aims-and-scope page on shop.elsevier.com, (G24) the full Guide for Authors as archived by
the Wayback Machine on 2024-03-15, and (LS) search-engine snippets of the live guide. Where G24 and LS differ, LS
is assumed current and the safer reading is chosen. Verify in Editorial Manager at submission.

## Traceability

- **Format freedom (page, body, headings, line numbers):** G24: Your Paper Your Way, one file "in any format or
  lay-out" at first submission; LS: "Save files in an editable format… .doc/.docx… .tex", "A PDF is not an
  acceptable source file", Word files single column. No font, spacing, margin or line-number rule is stated.
  Values above (US Letter, 1-inch margins, Times New Roman 12 pt, double spacing, continuous line numbers,
  page numbers) are a reviewer-friendly convention: **user decision 2026-11-27** (double spacing, line numbers, US Letter).
- **limits.abstract_words 250:** LS "does not exceed 250 words" (G24 states no limit).
- **limits.keywords 1-6:** G24 "a maximum of 6 keywords, using American spelling"; LS "1 to 7 keywords".
  The stricter (6) is used.
- **Highlights:** G24/LS "mandatory", "separate editable file" with "'Highlights' in the file name", "3 to 5
  bullet points (maximum 85 characters, including spaces, per bullet point)".
- **Graphical abstract:** G24 "mandatory"; separate file, "minimum of 531 × 1328 pixels (h × w)", readable at
  5 × 13 cm at 96 dpi, TIFF/EPS/PDF/MS Office.
- **references:** G24 "by number(s) in square brackets", "in the order in which they appear in the text";
  formatting not strict at submission. Rendered with the elsevier-with-titles CSL.
- **sections.required Conclusions:** G24 "Papers should contain a Conclusions section".
- **Data availability:** G24 "required to include a 'Data availability' section in their manuscript prior to
  their reference section" (LS: "encouraged"). Aims and scope (A): "Papers that are deemed to be primarily
  methodological but do not provide FAIR data and code will be returned without review."
- **CRediT:** G24 "we require corresponding authors to provide co-author contributions… using the relevant
  CRediT roles".
- **Competing interests:** G24 required "even if the authors have no competing interests to declare"; made
  with Elsevier's declarations tool and uploaded at Attach Files.
- **Generative AI:** G24 section "Declaration of Generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing
  process" at the end of the manuscript before the References, wording "During the preparation of this work the
  author(s) used [NAME TOOL / SERVICE] in order to [REASON]. After using this tool/service, the author(s)
  reviewed and edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the content of the
  publication." LS: heading now about "manuscript preparation process"; AI-generated images disclosed in
  captions too (none here: all figures are plotted from data by scripts).
- **Funding:** G24 wording if none: "This research did not receive any specific grant from funding agencies in
  the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors."
- **figures:** G24 EPS/PDF vector with embedded fonts, or TIFF/JPG ≥300 dpi halftone, ≥1000 dpi line art; LS
  adds PNG. Figures here are vector PDF (line art) with 600-dpi PNG copies.
- **Cover letter:** G24 suggests "three possible experts" as referees.
- **Scope (A):** "Validation of non-first-principles methods and transferability of methods must be included
  as part of each submission"; "Papers with a focus on simulations must contain new conceptual or computational
  advances".

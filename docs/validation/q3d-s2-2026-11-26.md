**Row 0.0123 is accepted against KR96's phase-field row.** It sits 8.8% above solvability in sigma*, as
KR96's own phase-field value does (+9.4%).

**Row 0.0265 (calibrated) lands 0.5% from solvability in sigma* and 1.0% in P.** It is not accepted
because of measurement, not error: at 11.2 cells across the tip, the tip-radius fit gives 9.3%
uncertainty and 4.1% drift in sigma*. The session-1 dx 0.6 control at the same row had 5.6%
uncertainty and 0.34% drift, so a calibrated dx 0.6 arm (about 1 h) would likely bring the budget
under 10%. Not run.

## Execution

- **fp64 box capacity.** At 16:20 no zone in us-east-1 had g6e.xlarge or g6e.2xlarge capacity.
  - A retry loop with a stop file got a g6e.2xlarge at 16:43.
  - The fp64 cap moved from $17 to $20, because 2xlarge costs more per hour. Spend stayed at $12.
- **ftz fix.** The first fp64 box failed all box gates: CuPy appends `-ftz=true` to every NVRTC build,
  and NVRTC rejects the port's duplicate `--ftz=false`.
  - Fix `78ba1c4`. FTZ affects only f32, and the f64 PTX is identical either way.
  - Relaunched at 16:53.
- **e123cal speed.** It ran at 5.7e9 voxel-steps/s (530 min), as the model's 522 min predicted. The
  0.84 speed-up factor seen on the smaller session-1 arms did not apply at 335M voxels.
- **Timeout guard.** With about 65 min of margin before the rows `timeout` (02:39), a guard was
  started on the box. It would pause that timeout if the arm were still running 5 min before it; it
  was never triggered.
- **Launcher death.** The local rows launcher died at 01:28 with exit 1 and no traceback (cause not
  found). The box finished on its own: ROWS_EXIT 0, REPLAY_EXIT 0. The final pull, PULLED and teardown
  were then done by hand with the launcher's own functions, ending at 01:45. No AWS leftovers.
- **Cost.** About $40.8: rows 9.4 h of g6e.4xlarge ($28.3) plus fp64 5.6 h of g6e.2xlarge ($12.4).

Growth

It grows in branches.

A metal crystal freezing out of its melt does not stay a ball. It sends arms out along the directions its lattice prefers.

Branching

Arms grow on arms.

Side arms sprout along each main arm, and smaller arms branch off the side arms, all along the same cube axes.

Cooling

Then it cools.

The render stops growing here. In a real casting the arms keep thickening and coarsening while melt remains.

The whole crystal

One seed, one crystal.

Every arm grew from the first speck, and all of them share one lattice.

    The third dimension

    TRUE 3D

    A cubic crystal grows six arms, not four. In the instrument you can section the volume, x-ray it for porosity and export it as an STL.

    The science

    No scripts. No particles.

    The crystal and the heat around it are two coupled fields, solved cell by cell on your GPU. Pour an alloy and a third field, the solute, joins them.

## Execution

  in any us-east-1 zone (twice); a g6e.xlarge (32 GB host RAM) where the e123base VRAM smoke failed with
  the reason unrecorded (diagnostics `1d3e9af`). The same smoke passed here (128 GB host RAM, 6.98 s).
- The 4xlarge's $35 cap (699 min) could not fit e123base after the other arms, so a local stopper sent
  SIGTERM to the rows run right after e265dx finished (06:03). ROWS_EXIT 143, REPLAY_EXIT 0.
- The harness status reads "refused or failed" with 3 errors: all three and the exception
  (TargetCloseError, detached frame) come from that forced stop. Consequence: the end-of-run GPU
  device-error ledger read was lost. During the four arms there were zero page or console errors, every
  arm passed its own status-flag checks, and the CPU replay matches every digit.
- A stray local retry loop (02:51-03:02) overwrote the local launch log and state file; it never
  launched a second instance (checked in AWS).
- Cost: about 3.5 h of g6e.4xlarge plus about 15 min of g6e.xlarge, roughly $11.

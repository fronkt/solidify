**Row 0.0265 is not accepted against either KR96 row.** At dx 0.6, with the anisotropy matched on that
grid, sigma* is 5.8% below solvability and P 3.4% above. Refining the grid cut the sigma* uncertainty
from 9.3% to 6.0% and the drift from 4.1% to 0.25%. The error, not the uncertainty, now keeps the
budget over 10%.

**The frozen prediction held.** From session-1 `e265dx` it gave sigma* 0.13343, P 0.25582, V d0/D 0.017465.
Measured minus predicted: sigma* +0.23%, P -0.13%, V d0/D -0.03%. Moving eps_eff from 0.026052 to 0.0265 at
dx 0.6 changed sigma* by +3.80%, P by -1.18% and V d0/D by +1.37%.

**Grid change at matched anisotropy** (`e265cal06` vs session-2 `e265cal`, both at eps_eff 0.0265 on their
own grid): sigma* -5.33%, P +2.37%, V d0/D -0.79%. This is within the 10% convergence rule
(`gridConverged` true). It also means the dx 0.8 arm's 0.5% agreement with solvability was partly the
coarse grid.
